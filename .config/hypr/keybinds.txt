===================================================================
                    HYPRLAND KEYBINDINGS GUIDE                     
===================================================================
Main Modifier (SUPER) = Windows Key
(Press any key to close this window)

--- APPLICATIONS & LAUNCHERS ---
SUPER + T                   Open Terminal (kitty)
SUPER + E                   Open File Manager (dolphin)
SUPER + SPACE               Open / Toggle App Launcher (wofi)
SUPER + R                   Open Menu (hyprlauncher)
SUPER + SHIFT + B           Reload / Restart Top Bar (waybar)
SUPER + M                   Open Power Menu (wlogout)

--- SCREENSHOTS (LUMINECAPTURE) ---
SUPER + P                   Capture active monitor
SUPER + SHIFT + P           Capture resizable region / window
SUPER + CTRL + P            Capture all monitors

--- WINDOW MANAGEMENT ---
SUPER + W                   Close active window
SUPER + F                   Toggle floating mode on window
SUPER + V                   Toggle split direction (dwindle)
SUPER + Left Mouse Drag     Move window
SUPER + Right Mouse Drag    Resize window

--- FOCUS & NAVIGATION (VIM KEYS) ---
SUPER + H                   Move focus Left
SUPER + L                   Move focus Right
SUPER + K                   Move focus Up
SUPER + J                   Move focus Down

--- MOVING WINDOWS (VIM KEYS) ---
SUPER + SHIFT + H           Move window Left
SUPER + SHIFT + L           Move window Right
SUPER + SHIFT + K           Move window Up
SUPER + SHIFT + J           Move window Down

--- WORKSPACES & SCRATCHPAD ---
SUPER + [1-9, 0]            Switch to workspace 1-10
SUPER + SHIFT + [1-9, 0]    Move active window to workspace 1-10
SUPER + Mouse Scroll        Cycle through workspaces
SUPER + S                   Toggle "magic" scratchpad workspace
SUPER + SHIFT + S           Move window to "magic" scratchpad

--- MULTIMEDIA & HARDWARE ---
Fn + . / ,                  Volume Up / Down (SwayOSD popup)
Fn + M                      Mute Audio (SwayOSD popup)
Waybar Center Clock Click   Toggle Notification Center (SwayNC)
Waybar Media Pill Click     Toggle Now Playing Dropdown
Waybar Audio / Clip Click   Toggle Audio / Clipboard Dropdowns
===================================================================
